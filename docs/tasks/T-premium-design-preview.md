## Requirements
- Cover every current `page.tsx` route, including administration and technical previews, with distinct relevant content.
- Egyptian Arabic RTL, existing brand identity, matte neutral surfaces and restrained lime actions.
- No decorative gradients, ubiquitous glass, fake social proof, fake product capabilities or dense card grids.
- All visible copy in `messages.ts`. Illustrative data stays local and is labeled as sample data.
- Native, labeled, at least 48px controls; working local navigation and representative interactions.
- Preview light and dark appearances and narrow viewports.
- Native public footer retains `© Gym visual`.
- No app, database, env or dependency changes, no commits or branches.
- Validate route coverage, local navigation, every rendered page, representative controls and mobile fit; run project check and build.

## Out of scope
Shipping the redesign, backend operations, real signup, payments, sending messages, modifying the existing player guide or application files.
