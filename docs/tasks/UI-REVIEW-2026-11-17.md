# UI review — kochivo.site, 2026-11-17

Pages reviewed:
- Live site in headless Edge, desktop (1366×850) and mobile (390×844), in Arabic and English, dark theme.
- Landing, login, privacy, terms and offline pages.
- The whole guided demo: 13 steps, captain and player views.

The automated check found no horizontal scrolling on any page, no buttons without a name, no broken images, no untranslated keys, and no console errors. Screenshots are in `ui-review-2026-11-17/`.

**Not covered:** the real captain and player screens after login. The sample account needs the Cloudflare check, which only a person can pass. That needs a separate pass, either with the owner's account or after the owner passes the check in the app's browser.
